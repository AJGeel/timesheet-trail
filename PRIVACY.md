# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Timesheet Trail is a browser extension that shows your browsing history by day and hour, and suggests how to fill in your timesheet. It is built to keep your data on your device.

## The short version

- Timesheet Trail **does not collect, transmit, sell or share** any personal data.
- Your browsing history is read **only on your device**, to display it and to compute suggestions.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## What the extension accesses

- **Browsing history.** Read through your browser's history API to list the pages you visited and when. It is not modified, deleted or sent anywhere.
- **Page icons (favicons).**
  - Chrome: read from Chrome's own local icon cache.
  - Firefox: the icon is requested from the website itself (its `/favicon.ico`), the same as when you visit it. No third-party service is involved.
- **Your settings.** Slot size, ticket sites, your manual adjustments to suggestions, and whether suggestions and update notices are shown. They are kept in your browser's local extension storage and never leave your device. They hold no browsing history.

## Tickets and suggestions

Ticket numbers (such as `ABC-123`) are detected from the titles and addresses of pages you visited, on the ticket sites you have chosen. This happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Third parties

Timesheet Trail sends no data to any server, ours or anyone else's. The only external link is "Report a bug or share feedback", which opens this project's issue page on GitHub in a new tab if you click it. GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) then applies to what you submit there.

## Your control

Your data stays in your browser. Uninstalling the extension removes its settings. Your browser's own history is managed in your browser and is not changed by Timesheet Trail.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
